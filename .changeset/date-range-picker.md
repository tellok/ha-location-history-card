Pick any range of days with Home Assistant's date range picker (presets, previous and next) instead of a single date and a time field. Trails, movement rows and playback cover the whole range: the timeline shows days when it spans more than one, times include the day, and playback speed scales with the number of days. The playhead starts at the end of the range, so every trail in it is drawn straight away; Play replays from the start. If the picker cannot load, the single date field is shown instead.
