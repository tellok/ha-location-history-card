Letting go of a playback range handle zooms the timeline (ticks, movement bars, playhead) and the map into the chosen stretch, and playback runs at the zoomed span's pace; the expand button zooms back out to the whole period. Time ticks go down to 5 minutes. The date range picker spans its row, with the previous / next arrows at the right.
